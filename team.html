Fly Close, Blaze of Glory

The old Icarus burned his wings chasing the sun and fell screaming into the sea. We are the Sons of Icarus: we strapped on the same wax and feathers, flew the same impossible climb — and kept our nerve when the sky turned to fire. Since 2025 we have soared from open qualifiers to the top of the region, threading the labyrinth with a four-player flight plan built on discipline, chemistry, and cold, unblinking audacity. The sun is still high, the wings still hold, and we are still climbing.
